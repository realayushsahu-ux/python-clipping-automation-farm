## What this channel is about
Gaming clips. The main game is GTA 6, but any game can come in (Minecraft, Valorant, BGMI, Free Fire...).
The viewer clicked because of THE GAME IN THE CLIP, so the title names that game within the first 40
characters, opened by a scroll-stopping first word, then ONE concrete thing (a role, mission, car, cop
chase, glitch, character, the streamer's name). Never put one game's name or hashtag on another game.

## Lessons from our first 6 Shorts (do not repeat)
- The only Short that took off (1,290 views) was clearly GTA 6 and named ONE concrete role:
  "GTA-6 BODYGUARD 😭". The 5 that stayed under 6 views were GTA 5 RP, or had vague titles
  ("GTA6 clips give goosebumps", "Speed loses it in GTA V RP", "speed got trolled by chat").
- Never vague words in a title: clips, moments, goosebumps, loses it, trolled, crazy.
- Never stuff hashtags or repeat them (#gta6 twice). 3-5 tags, the clip's game first.
- A moment everyone already clipped (a famous old stream) gets buried as a duplicate: prefer FRESH
  moments from new streams and moments that show something new in GTA 6.
- Credit the streamer, but the clip must make sense on its own and give the viewer a reason to stay:
  the on-screen hook says WHAT to watch for.

## Who we are talking to
One person: a 16-24 year old gamer scrolling Reels/Shorts at night, who knows GTA, follows streamers
like Speed, Kai Cenat, CarryMinati, Techno Gamerz, and swipes away in under 2 seconds if nothing
happens.

## How we talk
- GenZ gamer friend, not a news channel. Short, punchy, reacting WITH the viewer.
- English videos → simple English. Hindi/Hinglish videos → casual Hinglish in English letters
  ("Bhai ye kya ho gaya 😭"), never Devanagari.
- The streamer is the main character: use their name.
- One idea per line. Tease the payoff, never spoil it.

## Words we never use
"unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "you won't believe", "INSANE" / "VIRAL"
as filler, "like and subscribe" in the hook, "must watch", "shocking".

## Words we do use
bro, bhai, "did NOT", "wait for it", "POV:", 💀 😭 (max 2 emoji, never in the on-screen hook).

## What we will not claim
- No view counts, "viral", "million views", "trending #1" or any number not in the transcript.
- No fake news, no "GTA 6 leak" unless the clip really shows it.
- Never say the clip is ours: credit the streamer / original creator.

## Format
- 30-60 s vertical clips (35-45 s is our best so far), captions on screen, swear words censored (F**K).
- The first frame already shows the action the hook talks about: no slow intro, no black frames.
- On-screen hook: 3-7 words for the first 2.5 seconds, different words from what is spoken.
- Posts end with ONE question that makes people comment.
